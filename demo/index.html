Where the maps come from

Every height on this page is a measurement somebody else made. The files are the plugin's own (data/SOURCES.md has every product, its URL, its checksum and what was done to it); the globes are served here at half the plugin's resolution, made from its files by demo/tools/make_worlds.py.

  • Earth: NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information. 2022: ETOPO 2022 15 Arc-Second Global Relief Model. doi:10.25921/fd45-gt74. Public domain.
  • Moon: LRO Lunar Orbiter Laser Altimeter, LDEM_16 — NASA/GSFC/MIT, the LOLA team. Smith, D. E., et al. (2010), Space Sci. Rev. 150, 209–241. Public domain.
  • Mars: MGS Mars Orbiter Laser Altimeter, the MOLA DEM mosaic — NASA/GSFC MOLA Science Team; USGS Astrogeology Science Center. Smith, D. E., et al. (2001), J. Geophys. Res. 106(E10), 23689–23722. CC0 / public domain.
  • Mercury: MESSENGER Global DEM 665 m v2 — NASA/JHUAPL/Carnegie Institution of Washington/USGS. Becker, K. J., et al. (2016), 47th LPSC, abstract 2959. Public domain.
  • Venus: Magellan Global Topography 4641 m — NASA/JPL Magellan Team; P. Ford and G. Pettengill (MIT); USGS Astrogeology. Ford, P. G., & Pettengill, G. H. (1992), J. Geophys. Res. 97(E8), 13103–13114. Public domain.
  • Ceres and Vesta: Dawn Framing Camera HAMO global DTMs — NASA/JPL-Caltech/UCLA/MPS/DLR/IDA, via USGS Astrogeology. Preusker, F., et al. (2016), 47th LPSC, abstract 1954; Preusker, F., et al. (2014), LPI Contrib. 1773. No access or use constraints (NASA PDS).
  • Pluto and Charon: New Horizons LORRI-MVIC global DEMs, 300 m — NASA/JHUAPL/SwRI/LPI, the New Horizons team, via USGS Astrogeology. Schenk, P. M., et al. (2018), Icarus 314, 400–433; Schenk, P. M., et al. (2018), Icarus 315, 124–145. No access constraints (NASA).
  • The twenty-two places: Terrain Tiles on AWS Open Data — Mapzen / Tilezen (joerd), whose sources' licences require this attribution, copied verbatim from joerd's docs/attribution.md:
